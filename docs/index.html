Lead Characters

JUNO · 16

Pragmatic, grounded, and fiercely protective. The emotional core of the story, whose resilience drives her friends forward.

Wardrobe: worn vintage denim jacket over a faded band tee.

LYRA · 16

Juno’s brilliant, fast-talking best friend; a mythology scholar who treats ancient texts like puzzle boxes.

Key props: dog-eared reference books and a smartphone.

CALYPSO · looks 17

A sarcastic, intensely lonely Titan goddess concealing three thousand years of exile behind deadpan wit.

LETHE

The River of Forgetfulness made terrifyingly real: a memory-devouring antagonist with six tattered emerald wings.

MNEMOSYNE

Radiant Titan goddess of memory, clad in starlit silk and wielding legendary combat skills.

ACT I · INT. SCHOOL LIBRARY — AFTERNOON

Dust motes drift in the afternoon light. Lyra discovers that glowing ink in an old book forms a coordinate map. As she reads the inscription aloud, the library fractures into a vortex of ocean spray and celestial light.

JUNO
Lyra... what did you just read?!

LYRA
“A woodland isle, the navel of the sea, where dwells a goddess, daughter of Atlas of baneful mind.”

Earlier: Juno and Lyra argue over a mythology-heavy movie trailer in Lyra’s bedroom, establishing their friendship and contrasting personalities.

ACT II · EXT. OGYGIA — BEACH & JUNGLE

Juno and Lyra arrive on Ogygia and meet Calypso. A silver fog rolls over the island; Lethe feeds on memories. As the three flee through the jungle, Lyra realizes the ancient book is Calypso’s journal—and the incantation was a distress signal.

CALYPSO
I haven't seen a text portal open since the Bronze Age. Usually the Fates just toss me shipwrecked sailors.

JUNO
No one gets used to being alone. We’re getting you off this island.

CALYPSO
That’s my handwriting. It’s my journal. I wrote that incantation and cast it into the Aegean Sea three thousand years ago like a message in a bottle.

ACT III · INT. CRYSTAL CAVES — DUSK

Lethe attacks in the crystal caves. Juno resists the memory fog by holding onto a memory of her mother; Lyra invokes Mnemosyne, who arrives in a flash of gold and defeats Lethe. With the fog gone, the friends learn Calypso’s exile has ended.

JUNO
Her name... her name is Sarah... She makes burnt pancakes every Sunday... she puts cinnamon in the batter!

MNEMOSYNE
I am Mnemosyne. Try not to forget it.

EPILOGUE · INT. HIGH SCHOOL HALLWAY — DAY

Back at school, Calypso is adjusting to modern life—and unimpressed by P.E. Juno and Lyra welcome her into their circle.

CALYPSO
I fought the Minotaur's cousin in the Bronze Age. I am not playing dodgeball with a boy named Chad.

JUNO
Come on. Let's show you where the cafeteria is. Just... don't smite the lunch ladies if the pizza is cold.

FADE OUT.
